Apple Stock Price Analysis &
Forecasting

Analyzed historical Apple stock data to identify trends and build predictive models. Compared ARIMA and lag-based regression approaches and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize model performance and forecast future prices.

Key Insights

  • Stock prices show a strong upward trend over time
  • Lag1 model achieved the lowest MSE (~16.39)
  • Lag-based models outperformed ARIMA for short-term prediction
  • Predicted values closely follow actual prices
  • Forecast indicates short-term price movement

Models Used

  • ARIMA (0,1,0), (1,1,0), (0,1,1), (1,1,1)
  • Lag-based Linear Regression (Lag1, Lag2, Lag3)
  • Linear Regression

Tools & Technologies

  • Python (Pandas, NumPy, statsmodels, scikit-learn)
  • Power BI

Impact

Improved short-term stock price prediction by identifying lag-based models as more accurate than ARIMA, supporting better forecasting decisions.

England & Wales Property
Price Analysis

This project analyzes property price trends across England and Wales using transaction-level data. A Tableau dashboard was developed to explore regional price variations, property types, and market activity over time.

Key Insights

  • London properties are significantly more expensive than other regions
  • Existing properties are priced higher than new builds in most regions
  • Detached houses have the highest average prices among property types
  • Freehold transactions dominate the market compared to leasehold
  • Property prices show long-term growth with fluctuations over time

Dashboard Highlights

  • Regional price comparison between London and outside London
  • Price distribution by property type (Detached, Semi-Detached, Terrace, Flat)
  • Geographical visualization of most and least expensive counties
  • Trend analysis of property prices over years
  • Quarterly transaction patterns by tenure

Tools & Technologies

  • SQL (Data extraction & transformation)
  • Tableau (Dashboard & visualization)

Impact

Revealed regional price disparities and long-term market trends, supporting better understanding of property investment patterns across England and Wales.

Energy Consumption
Prediction using Bayesian
Logistic Regression

This project analyzes energy consumption patterns in Tetuan City and builds a predictive model using Bayesian Logistic Regression. A Random Walk Metropolis (MCMC) algorithm was implemented to estimate model parameters and predict the probability of high energy consumption based on temperature.

Key Insights

  • Energy consumption increases with temperature
  • Clear relationship between temperature and high energy usage
  • Bayesian model effectively captures uncertainty in predictions
  • Model achieves good classification performance on test data
  • Predictive probabilities help estimate future energy demand

Modeling Approach

  • Data preprocessing and feature engineering
  • Binary classification based on mean energy consumption
  • Bayesian Logistic Regression
  • Random Walk Metropolis (MCMC) for parameter estimation
  • Model evaluation using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score

Model Visualization

The logistic regression curve shows the relationship between temperature and probability of high energy consumption.

Predicted probabilities indicate increasing likelihood of high energy consumption with rising temperature.

Tools & Technologies

  • R Programming
  • Statistical Modeling (Bayesian Inference)
  • Data Visualization

Impact

Developed a probabilistic model to predict high energy consumption, enabling better understanding of demand patterns and uncertainty in energy usage.

UK Rail Performance & Delay
Analysis

This project analyzes UK railway journey data to uncover insights into travel demand, delays, and revenue patterns. The analysis combines SQL, Python, and Tableau to create a fully interactive dashboard.

Key Features

  • Interactive Tableau Dashboard
  • Time-based travel pattern analysis
  • Delay rate and delay cause analysis
  • Revenue insights by station
  • Customer ticket behavior analysis

Tools Used

  • SQL (MySQL)
  • Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
  • Tableau

Key Insights

  • Peak travel occurs during morning and evening hours
  • Delay rates increase during high-demand periods
  • Revenue is concentrated in major stations
  • Advance tickets dominate customer purchases

Impact

Identified peak delay periods and key causes such as weather and signal failures, enabling data-driven insights for improving scheduling and operational efficiency.
